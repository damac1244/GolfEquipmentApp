Why this is hard

Used clubs aren't products. They're one-offs.

Every listing is different

Ten shops sell the identical new driver. No two used ones match — different wear, different shaft, different story. Lining them up is the whole problem.

“Excellent” means five things

One seller's Excellent is another's Good. We map each seller's grades onto one scale, and say what the words actually describe: face wear, sky marks, shaft ticks.

The real number is the total

A cheaper club with $29 shipping isn't cheaper. Every price here is what leaves your account, ranked on that and nothing else.
