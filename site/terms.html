Terms of use

The short version: Bagdrop shows you prices other people are asking. It is not the seller, and the price on the seller's own page is the one that counts.

Last updated 5 October 2026

Bagdrop is not a shop

Bagdrop does not sell goods, hold stock, process payment, or ship anything. Every purchase is a contract between you and the seller you click through to. Their terms, returns policy, warranty and delivery terms apply, not Bagdrop's.

Prices are a snapshot, not a quote

Prices are collected from public listings once a day. Every listing on the site shows when it was last confirmed, and some are several days old. A price may have changed, the item may have sold, and shipping or taxes may differ at checkout, particularly across a border.

Check the price on the seller's own page before you buy. Bagdrop makes no guarantee that any price shown is current, available, or complete.

How results are ordered

Results are ranked by the total price including shipping where the seller states it, and by nothing else. No seller can pay for placement. Where shipping is not stated it is marked as unknown rather than assumed to be free.

Listings the site cannot compare like for like are flagged and excluded from the headline figures. This is a judgement made by software and it will sometimes be wrong in both directions.

Affiliate links

Bagdrop intends to earn a commission on some purchases made through its outbound links. That commission is paid by the seller and costs you nothing. It does not affect which sellers appear or in what order.

Acceptable use

You are welcome to use the site to compare prices. Please do not scrape it in bulk, republish its data as your own, or attempt to disrupt it. The price data is assembled by Bagdrop; the trademarks, product names and images belong to their respective owners and appear here to identify the products being compared.

No warranty

The site is provided as it is, without guarantees of accuracy or availability. It is run as a side project by one person. To the extent the law allows, Bagdrop is not liable for any loss arising from reliance on the prices shown. Nothing here limits rights you have under consumer law that cannot be limited.
